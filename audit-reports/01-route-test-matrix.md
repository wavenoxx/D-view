## 3. Detailed Route-Group Observations

### 3.1 Homepage (`/`)
- **Heading Defect (`SEO-05`):** Contains **8 `<h1>` tags** in the DOM: 1 hidden `<h1 class="sr-only">` from `src/pages/index.astro` (line 32) and **7 `<h1>` tags** rendered inside the mobile overlay cards of `CITY_SLIDES` in `src/components/DViewWebsite.tsx` (line 2148: `<h1>Invisible Grills in {slide.cityShort}</h1>`). On desktop (`1440×900`), **0 `<h1>` tags are visible** (the desktop slide title uses `<h2>` on line 2222 while mobile uses `<h1>` on line 2148). On mobile (`390×844`), all **7 mobile slide `<h1>` tags** report as visible in the DOM carousel track.
- **Privacy & Lead Modal Defect (`PRIV-01`, `FORM-06`):** Clicking `CONTACT US`, `Book Free Laser Survey`, `Book a Free Site Visit`, or `Request Similar Setup` opens the lead modal (`isModalOpen`), which immediately embeds `customerName` and `customerPhone` into `https://api.qrserver.com/v1/create-qr-code/?size=140x140&data=...` on every keystroke and allows empty/invalid phone numbers on `main`.

### 3.2 Core Pages Using `Navbar.tsx` (`/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/sitemap`, `/solutions`, `/projects`, `/locations`)
- **Broken Same-Page Navigation Links (`NAV-01`):** `src/components/Navbar.tsx` uses bare relative hashes (`#solutions`, `#projects`, `#faq`, `#book-visit`) instead of full paths:
  - On `/about`, `/privacy`, `/sitemap`, `/projects`, and `/locations`: `#projects`, `#faq`, and `#book-visit` (both desktop and mobile CTA buttons) are **100% broken dead links** (target IDs do not exist on those pages). Even `#solutions` only accidentally matches the desktop dropdown trigger wrapper `<div id="solutions">` inside `Navbar.tsx` itself (line 135)!
  - On `/contact` and `/solutions`: `#book-visit` exists (in `SiteVisitForm.tsx`), but `#projects` and `#faq` are broken dead links, and clicking `Solutions` (`#solutions`) does not scroll to the services grid on `/solutions` because `ServicesGrid.astro` uses `id="services"` (line 43).

## 3. Written Evidence Gate for Future Marketing Claims

Before any of the following commercial claims can be added to the live website, Google Ads, or advertising campaigns, the business operator must furnish the corresponding written evidence:

### Gate A: Written Warranty Policy Document
- **Proposed Claim:** "5-Year Comprehensive Warranty"
- **Required Written Evidence:**
  1. A formal, written 1-page Warranty Terms & Conditions document (PDF) detailing:
     - Covered items (e.g., cable tensile failure, mounting track detachment, material defect).
     - Excluded items (e.g., intentional wire cutting, building structural movement, third-party facade renovation).
     - Customer claim procedure (contact email/phone, service response SLA).
     - Sample warranty card issued with final invoice.
- **Current Status:** *Pending operator drafting.*

### Gate B: Metallurgical & Load Test Certificates
- **Proposed Claim:** Specific breaking load (e.g. 1500 N, breaking tension), salt spray resistance hours, or specific alloy grade certifications.
- **Required Written Evidence:**
  1. Mill test certificates from the wire rope manufacturer (Grade SS-316 / SS-304).
  2. NABL-accredited laboratory test report (e.g. tensile pull test, chemical spectrometer analysis).
- **Current Status:** *Not required under Step 10A conservative language; required only if restoring specific load or lab figures.*

### Gate C: Standardized Commercial Rate Card
- **Proposed Claim:** Fixed ₹/sq.ft rates (e.g., ₹135/sq.ft) or itemized installation charges.
- **Required Written Evidence:**
  1. An official, approved price schedule signed by the business operator defining:
     - Base rate per square foot for SS-304 (2.5mm / 3.0mm).
     - Base rate per square foot for SS-316 (2.5mm / 3.0mm).
     - Track mounting options (powder-coated vs. anodized aluminum).
     - Minimum billable area threshold (if any).
     - Outstation / inter-city travel charges for AP corridors.
- **Current Status:** *Not active. Calculators truthfully route customers to formal quotations.*

### Gate D: Physical AP Hub Documentation
- **Proposed Claim:** "Local installation crew based in Visakhapatnam / Vijayawada" or "Regional Headquarters in AP".
- **Required Written Evidence:**
  1. Physical commercial office lease, warehouse agreement, or local depot address in Andhra Pradesh.
  2. GST registration certificate showing AP branch / additional place of business (if applicable).
  3. Local full-time technician roster or verified local installation partner contract.
- **Current Status:** *AP operations are currently framed honestly as regional expansion planning.*

### Gate E: Documented Customer Installations
- **Proposed Claim:** Customer testimonials, case studies, or named society portfolio.
- **Required Written Evidence:**
  1. Written or digital consent from the property owner to photograph and display their balcony.
  2. Invoice copy or job card matching the location and installation date.
- **Current Status:** *Current imagery is honestly presented as architectural reference layouts.*
